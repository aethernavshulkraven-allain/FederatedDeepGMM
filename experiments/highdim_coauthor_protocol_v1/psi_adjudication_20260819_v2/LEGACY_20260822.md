## Status

`results/highdim_psi_adjudication_20260819_v2/` are **retired as scientific
evidence**. They are not deleted, moved, or edited — every completed and

## Why

The server-side federated-optimizer update (`FedAvgAPI`'s predictor/
corrector, OGDA, and plain-SGD server steps) applied learning-rate
interpolation or OGDA optimistic extrapolation to the **entire** model
`state_dict`, not just trainable parameters. For any model with BatchNorm
(every FEMNIST/CIFAR10 critic in this campaign), that silently extrapolated
`running_var` and `num_batches_tracked` every round. This could and did
drive a valid running variance negative, producing a nonfinite evaluation
downstream — reproduced directly from the flagged `femnist_z/fedogda_d`
seed-1 (lr=0.001, cm=10) trajectory in this v2 signal manifest.

Full root cause, the fix (`server_buffer_policy=direct_client_aggregate`,
parameter-only server updates, direct aggregation for buffers, deterministic
max for integer counters), and the replacement campaign are documented in
`../psi_adjudication_20260822_v3/CORRECTION_ADDENDUM_20260822.md`.

and `LEGACY_20260822_ADDENDUM.md` in the finals directory for the parallel
